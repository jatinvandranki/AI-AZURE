RESEARCH SUMMARY
================

DATA
Source: Microsoft Azure Public Dataset V2 (2019)
CPU shards used: 1
Raw records: 10000000
Unique VMs: 241490
Intervals: 45; training: 31; testing: 14

METHOD
Two cooperating Q-learning agents control bounded server capacity and consolidation. The 70/30 split is chronological. Energy is estimated with P(u)=P_idle+(P_max-P_idle)u and integrated over 5-minute intervals. SLA violation means response_ms > 200; compliance is 1 - violation.

AI mean/max overload: 0.93036/1.26401

STATISTICS
Interval-level paired tests were used; seed-level aggregate t-tests were not used. Holm-adjusted p-values: [1.3785266451162783e-12, 1.0338949838372087e-12, 1.316260732484134e-12, 6.58130366242067e-13]

LIMITATIONS
Energy is modeled, not measured Azure data-center power. The local workspace currently contains only the shards reported above; a short trace and few held-out intervals limit statistical power. Results should be interpreted as a trace-driven simulation study, not a production deployment claim.
